## Primary sources checked

- [Google Flow Help](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16353333?hl=en) describes its generation tools, SynthID marking and linked policies. [Google's US terms](https://policies.google.com/terms?hl=en-US) say Google does not claim ownership of generated original content. The [Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy](https://policies.google.com/terms/generative-ai/use-policy) prohibits misuse and deceptive provenance claims. The site retains the original scene files and discloses their generated nature. These sources do not certify any particular image's rights or give exclusive ownership.
- [OpenAI's content terms](https://openai.com/policies/row-terms-of-use/) assign OpenAI's rights, if any, in output to the user to the extent permitted by law, while warning that outputs may not be unique and that other rights must be respected. This is not a guarantee of copyright protection or brand clearance.
- [Microsoft Copilot terms](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/for-individuals/termsofuse) and [Bing Image Creator terms](https://www.bing.com/new/termsofuseimagecreator) were read after the supplied emblem was identified as likely Copilot-generated. Different Microsoft products and account types have different terms. Rather than treating Azure metadata as an asset-specific commercial licence, the new pages use their existing vector brand symbol. No change is made to the live site's legacy artwork.

The review did not cryptographically validate content-credential signatures, inspect generation-account records or determine which historic agreement governed each creation. Joseph remains responsible for supplied-material rights and final publication decisions. No trademark registration, customer endorsement, actual trial location or exclusive copyright in wholly generated imagery is claimed by these notes.
