THE EVERYDAY PROBLEM

Training shouldn’t mean
explaining everything from scratch.

A delivery arrives. A new colleague needs help. Your most experienced person stops to explain the same task again.

New hires learn at different speeds, and much of the know-how lives in people’s heads. Small teams need a way to share it while the day’s work keeps moving.

Know My Business is designed for hands-on work: a short check-in before practice, learning beside a mentor, and a quick reflection afterward. The work stays out on the floor, not inside a course all day.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Three parts.
One learning relationship.

Frontier AI + Adaptive Learning + Human Mentorship. AI helps prepare the path; the mentor and trainee put it into practice and adjust it together.

Frontier AI

Turn a mentor’s explanation into a draft learning path. Optional drafting uses models from OpenAI or Anthropic, the makers of ChatGPT and Claude. The mentor checks the content before it is used.

Adaptive Learning

Start with what this person can do: not yet, with help, or independently. The mentor uses observed practice and questions to choose the next step and adjust the support.

Human Mentorship

Your experienced people remain the teachers. They demonstrate the work, review AI suggestions and decide when a trainee is ready for more independence.

This is the product’s design approach, not a validated training method. AI provider names identify optional technology, not partnerships or endorsements.
