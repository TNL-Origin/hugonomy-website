HUMAN KNOW-HOW+AI-ASSISTED PREPARATION→ROOM FOR PEOPLE TO GROW

01 / THE EVERYDAY OPPORTUNITY

Small teams hold
a lot of knowledge.

How do you turn “let me show you” into a learning path a new colleague can follow?

In a food shop, knowledge lives in people, routines and conversations. An experienced colleague knows what to demonstrate, what needs supervised practice and when to ask for help.

SEBANORI is being built to help that mentor make the path explicit. AI helps organize a draft. The mentor brings the context, edits the plan and decides what is ready.

✳

An initial focus on food retail.
Exploring the needs of owner-led shops and small teams. We welcome conversations about future prototype testing; no workplace pilot, customer partnership or performance result is being announced.

03 / THE HUGONOMY THREAD

Build capability.
Keep agency.

Explore Hugonomy’s philosophy ↗

People should remain active participants in the work they do with AI.

Hugonomy’s wider work asks how technology can support human awareness, judgment and intentional action. SEBANORI brings that question into learning at work.

The ambition is practical: help a mentor prepare a better learning path, give the trainee room to ask questions and practise, and keep approval grounded in human observation.

Human augmentation is the design goal. Whether SEBANORI improves learning in real workplaces is a question to test, not an outcome already established.

01 / PREPARE

The mentor shapes the tree.

Describe the department and specific skills. Review an AI-assisted draft, edit its steps and prerequisites, then explicitly approve the learning plan.

02 / PRACTISE

A focus for today.

See the approved focus, skill marks and learning orb. The trainee and mentor share a view of the current plan, with the underlying detail available.

03 / REFLECT

Keep the conversation open.

Save reflections and deliberately send questions or concerns through an in-app inbox. AI suggestions remain drafts for human review.

↳

What the orb means: current recorded learning support, represented through color and shape. It is not a personality profile, productivity score or certified assessment of competence.

05 / WHERE THINGS STAND

Early. Tangible.
Open to conversation.

We’re seeking businesses interested in helping test the prototype, and conversations with potential investors who share our interest in human-centered learning. Participation would be discussed and agreed individually.

PRIVATE PROTOTYPE

Mentor-led learning-tree setup, optional Claude or OpenAI drafting with a mentor-supplied API key kept in that browser for private demonstration only, skill marks and orb, reflections, and a local in-app inbox. The private app demonstration uses fictional sample data and a role-switching mode so visitors can explore the mentor and trainee experiences. Workplace testing is a proposed next step, subject to the necessary agreements and safeguards.

EXPLORING NEXT

Multiple trainees per mentor, carefully scoped supervisor access and mentor-selected document connections. These are proposed directions, not working product integrations.

BEFORE REAL USE

Authorization, secure accounts, appropriate privacy and records controls, independent review and legal guidance. No confirmed workplace-testing start date, public launch date, pricing or proven business return is being announced.
