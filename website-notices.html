The working prototype and the website walkthrough

SEBANORI is a working application prototype being developed for mentor-led workplace learning. The private app includes mentor-reviewed learning trees, skill marks, reflections and an in-app inbox. Its optional live AI drafting uses a mentor-supplied API key held in that browser for the private demonstration.

The walkthrough on this website is a separate, template-based introduction using fictional people and sample information. You can click through it without an account. It does not connect to live AI, upload files or save learning records. Some screens illustrate planned integrations and interface adaptation; those are labeled as proposed features. The working app is still being developed, and real workplace use will require further security work, testing and agreed permissions.

AI output can contain errors. These pages do not provide individualized legal, medical, financial or workplace-safety advice. The founder's credentials do not create a professional relationship. Prototype demonstrations are not safety certifications or tools for employment decisions. Real workplace use requires secure infrastructure, authorization, appropriate human review and separately agreed data practices.

Privacy on this website

This website is planned to use two tools to count visits and measure speed: Google Analytics and Netlify's real-user monitoring. Google Analytics uses cookies and identifiers to measure how visitors use a site, such as which pages are viewed and roughly where visitors are. Google says Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses, although IP addresses are still used to run the service and to estimate location. Netlify's monitoring measures page-speed figures (Core Web Vitals) and, according to Netlify, needs no cookies on the visitor's browser. We use this information to understand traffic and improve the site.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser, and Google offers a browser add-on that turns off Google Analytics measurement. Web hosting services also keep ordinary technical records of requests. Following an external link takes you to that destination's services and policies.

The sample walkthrough does not save your entries in your browser or send them to an AI provider. Contact choices change an email link locally; choosing a category does not send a message. If you send an email, your email provider and the recipient's email system process that message. Do not send passwords, API keys, confidential business documents, employee records or other sensitive information merely to request a conversation.

These new page files do not yet include a Google Analytics tag. Netlify may add monitoring through the host. The final analytics and consent configuration must be confirmed before publication. The existing live Systems website already uses analytics and a mailing-list form. Its FoldSpace extension privacy policy is specific to that extension; it is not the privacy notice for Business or this proposed shared website.
