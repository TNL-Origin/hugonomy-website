The working prototype and the website walkthrough

SEBANORI is a working application prototype being developed for mentor-led workplace learning. The private app includes mentor-reviewed learning trees, skill marks, reflections and an in-app inbox. Its optional live AI drafting uses a mentor-supplied API key held in that browser for the private demonstration.

The walkthrough on this website is a separate, template-based introduction using fictional people and sample information. You can click through it without an account. It does not connect to live AI, upload files or save learning records. Some screens illustrate planned integrations and interface adaptation; those are labeled as proposed features. The working app is still being developed, and real workplace use will require further security work, testing and agreed permissions.

AI output can contain errors. These pages do not provide individualized legal, medical, financial or workplace-safety advice. The founder's credentials do not create a professional relationship. Prototype demonstrations are not safety certifications or tools for employment decisions. Real workplace use requires secure infrastructure, authorization, appropriate human review and separately agreed data practices.

Privacy and optional analytics

This notice covers the shared landing page, the Systems introduction, Hugonomy Business, the SEBANORI website walkthrough and this notice page. Google Analytics is off on these pages until you choose "Accept Google Analytics". Rejecting is an equal option. Use "Analytics choices" in the footer to change your choice. Global Privacy Control keeps optional Google Analytics off.

After acceptance, Google Analytics receives page-visit, browser/device and approximate location information and may set analytics cookies to distinguish visits. Google uses IP-address information to estimate approximate location and operate its analytics service. Google's published IP-handling practices vary by region and, for linked properties, Google Ads settings. See Google's regional privacy guidance and IP-address guidance for details. Our configuration disables Google advertising signals and ad-personalization signals. We store your choice in this browser for up to 180 days and configure analytics-cookie expiry to the same maximum. Changing an existing choice reloads the page and resets the sample walkthrough. Withdrawal stops this site's Google Analytics tag on these pages and clears accessible Google Analytics cookies; it does not automatically delete information already processed by Google.

Netlify hosts this website and can keep technical request records. Its real-user monitoring measures page performance and does not require browser cookies. Hosting logs and Netlify performance monitoring are separate from the optional Google Analytics choice. The website operator is Joseph D. Tingling; contact joseph@hugonomy.com for privacy questions or requests.

The fictional walkthrough keeps entries in page memory, not saved learning records, and sends nothing to an AI provider. The analytics choice is the separate browser-storage item described above. Selecting a contact category only changes an email link. Email is sent only when you send it in your email app, using your email provider and the recipient's system. Do not send passwords, API keys, confidential documents or employee records to request a conversation.

The existing FoldSpace product and other legacy Systems pages keep their existing analytics and mailing-list integrations; the choices on these new shared pages do not control those older pages. The FoldSpace extension privacy policy describes the browser extension, not analytics on the website. Review a destination's own terms and practices when following a link.
